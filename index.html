TrackCleaner

Radio-clean edits, on your Mac. It ducks only the vocal over each curse word while the music keeps playing — then hands you a waveform and a transcript so you can check every censor by ear.

Download for Apple Silicon All releases

You need an activation key

TrackCleaner is licensed software and stays locked until a key is entered. Keys are issued to individual people at BPM Supreme — ask your team lead if you have not been sent one.

Installing

  1. Download the .dmg above.
  2. Open it and drag TrackCleaner to your Applications folder.
  3. Launch it and paste in your activation key.

It opens like any other Mac app — the build is signed and notarized by Apple. If macOS warns you about it, the download is incomplete; get a fresh copy rather than working around the warning.

The first launch needs an internet connection and a few minutes while it downloads the audio models it uses, with progress shown. After that it works offline.

How it works

TrackCleaner separates the vocal from the instrumental, lines the words up to the audio, and finds the profanity. Where it finds a word it lowers the vocal for exactly that moment, so the beat and the music carry straight through — a real radio edit rather than a bleep or a gap.

It listens to a song more than once, and it tells you where it is unsure instead of quietly guessing: near-misses, unclear moments and stretches where nothing was heard all end up in a short "worth a listen" checklist you can play through. Nothing is exported that you have not reviewed.

Songs never leave your Mac. All processing is local.
